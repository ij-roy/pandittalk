# Brand and human experience specification

## Brand foundation — retain the founder's words

> Pandit Talk stands for — Ek Soch. Ek Sach. Ek Koshish.
> Predict. Prepare. Prosper.
> We don't believe astrology should create fear or sell false promises. Aapka Karma aapko hi karna padega, par direction hum dikha sakte hain.
> Past has gone. Present is yours. And the choices you make today can shape a better tomorrow.
> We don't claim that “yeh hoga hi.” We help you understand the possibilities, prepare for them and find the right direction.
> Hum business nahi, hum aapke liye hain.
> Traditional wisdom, generational Pandits and modern technology—brought together with one simple belief: when you talk, you deserve to be heard.
> No celebrity noise. No unrealistic promises. Bas sach, samajh aur sahi direction.
> Try once. Experience it yourself. Then decide.
> Pandit Talk — You Talk. We Stay.

Use the complete statement on Our Approach, with shorter excerpts elsewhere. “Hum business nahi” is the founder's expression of service intent; surround it with transparent pricing and business identity so it cannot imply a free/nonprofit service. “Generational” is a practitioner-specific claim requiring evidence. “We Stay” means a dependable experience and clear support path, not unlimited free access or 24/7 staffing unless actually provided.

## Required first screen: why Pandit Talk

Use this hierarchy:

1. Introductory label: **Why Pandit Talk?**
2. Primary heading: **Traditional Wisdom + Real Pandits + Modern Technology**
3. Clear choice: **Scheduled Audio or Video** / **Chat Now when available**.
4. Supporting line: **See the duration, total and available time before you decide.**
5. Explanation: **The booking service keeps the terms clear from the first choice to the final minute.**
6. Sign-off: **You Talk. We Stay.**
7. Primary action: **Explore Pandits**. Secondary action: **How our conversations work**.

Keep the heading, differentiator and primary action visible in a representative 390 × 844 initial viewport at normal text settings. At 360px or enlarged text, allow natural scrolling rather than shrinking or clipping text. Place the portrait below the copy on mobile. The older “Aapki baat. Dhyaan se.” becomes supporting editorial copy; the manifesto and “Predict. Prepare. Prosper.” remain in supporting brand moments.

### Visually distinctive composition

Desktop: an asymmetrical two-column opening, with roughly three-fifths devoted to typography and the remainder to one consented real-Pandit portrait. Break the three heading ideas into deliberate lines with restrained ochre accents. Give the scheduled and Chat Now choices a clear, compact action block with duration and total context. Do not bury the real next step in a badge or carousel.

Use warm ivory, deep ink, restrained saffron and natural photography. Avoid three generic feature cards, fake chat screenshots, ticking clocks, progress rings, rotating slogans and autoplay video. Optional short entrance fades must respect reduced motion and never delay readable content. On mobile preserve the exact semantic reading order and comfortable type size.

### Required section: How timed conversations work

Place a dedicated section directly after the opening introduction, before large directory/tool sections. Explain the two paths through **Choose a time. Or check who is available now.** Pair calm imagery with links to how conversations work, current pricing and cancellation/refund terms.

The offer must show the backend-provided duration and total before confirmation. The server controls the countdown and end time; the browser cannot extend or invent the session. Scheduled Audio/Video and Chat Now remain separate formats, with availability, queue, cancellation and refund behavior explained in plain language. Do not promise outcomes, hidden free time or a different timer model. Existing bookings retain their stored terms.

### Required section: your story continues

Heading: **Your story shouldn't have to start from zero every time.**

Supporting copy: **Keep what matters from your last conversation. Choose what you want to share, and pick up from there.**

Show a simple editorial sequence: **Your words → Your saved context → Your next conversation**; stack it vertically on mobile. An illustrative user-authored note may read “Last time, I wanted to talk about a change at work.” Label it as an example, never as a testimonial or practitioner-written summary. Action: **How your story stays with you**, linking to the continuity explanation and privacy controls.

Required backing: optional private saved context, review/edit/delete, explicit selection of context and recipient for a consultation, and an equally clear **Start fresh** choice. The selected Pandit sees only deliberately shared context. Allow rebooking the same person when available, without promising perpetual availability or human memory. No automatic recording, transcription, AI memory or silent sharing with every Pandit. Design this section now; publish persistent-memory claims only when storage, permissions and practitioner access work end to end.

## Voice

Calm, direct, warm. Speak to adults with agency. Use natural English with intentional Hindi/Hinglish moments; avoid switching language midway through critical transaction instructions. Prepare separate reviewed Hindi messages for errors, consent and payment details. Match the user's selected language across a journey; if full translation is unavailable, do not display a misleading language switch.

Suggested original copy:

- Hero: **Traditional Wisdom + Real Pandits + Modern Technology**. Choice: **Scheduled Audio/Video or Chat Now when available.** Sign-off: **You Talk. We Stay.**
- Brand signature: “Ek Soch. Ek Sach. Ek Koshish.” Secondary line: “Predict. Prepare. Prosper.”
- Choice: “What would you like to talk about?”
- Notes: “Anything you would like your Pandit to know? Share only what feels relevant.”
- Uncertainty: “You don't need to have the perfect question.”
- Empty availability: “No suitable time right now. Choose another date or explore another Pandit.”
- Payment pending: “Your payment is still being checked. You can check its status here.”
- Automation label: “An automated interpretation, not a personal consultation with a Pandit.”
- Closing: “Try once. Experience it yourself. Then decide.” No free-trial implication without an actual approved offer.

Avoid guaranteed success, curse-removal pressure, diagnostic language, lucky-number certainty, artificial countdowns, “most chosen” without data, fake wait queues and pseudo-personal greetings suggesting a human has read data when they have not.

## Visual direction

Recommended direction: a thoughtful Indian editorial service, with real faces and practical product clarity. Retain recognizable logo assets and the app's warm ivory/gold direction. Build a quiet cream background, deep readable ink, restrained dark saffron/ochre accent and neutral dividers. Use gold for decoration only where contrast is insufficient for text. Validate exact tokens against WCAG contrast before adoption.

Typography: one readable body family supporting Latin/Devanagari and one restrained editorial heading family if licensing and font budget permit. System-font fallback must remain attractive. Avoid six weights, tiny all-caps navigation and large vague statements repeated on every page.

Composition: varied editorial sections with deliberate whitespace. Home leads with a real human image and direct invitation; directory is comparative and practical; profile is personal; consultation room is functional and quiet; reports emphasize structured reading. Do not reuse the same rounded card grid for every page.

Avoid floating gradient blobs, gratuitous glass panels, starfield wallpaper, golden glow on every control, spinning zodiac heroes, animated text carousels, fabricated dashboards, AI-generated testimonial portraits, generic stock call-centre photographs and decorative autoplay audio. Existing `Lightfall` and old chanting assets are not a requirement to preserve in the primary experience.

## Asset production brief

Request practitioner portraits with written publication consent: natural light, eye-level framing, real setting, consistent crop and no invented religious props. Collect name pronunciation, languages, self-described practice, teaching lineage where verifiable, years of practice with evidence and two genuine answers: “What do people usually ask you?” and “What can someone expect in a conversation?”

Use actual app screenshots after removing personal data. Illustrate processes with simple typography/icons rather than synthetic proof of customer success. Maintain an asset record containing origin, permission, allowed use, expiry if any, alt text and subject approval. No real assets means a graceful typographic layout until approved assets arrive.

## Differentiators with explicit scope

### Required: know the person before booking

Publish real introductions, clear language/practice and verification explanation. A short introduction recording with transcript is optional and depends on approved media plus CMS fields. No autoplay. Available session formats and availability must be server-backed.

### Required: understand the commitment

Before paying, display person, topic, format, agreed scope/completion rules, start time/timezone, total coins, available balance, INR package purchase if recharge is needed, and cancellation link. No forced “recommended” top-up. Server reconfirms after price changes.

### Required: human support after something goes wrong

Session/payment/report screens link to contextual help using opaque IDs, not sensitive conversation excerpts. Show the actual state and next action. Never promise a refund before the backend or support decision confirms it.

### Proposed extension: prepare in your own words

Optional question sheet: “What is on your mind?”, “What would make this conversation useful?”, “What have you already tried?” One short text field per question; skip always available. Initially integrate with existing private booking notes only where size and purpose fit. Persistent separate drafts require a new private data contract and consent/retention rules.

### Proposed extension: your next step

Private reflection after a session: “What did I understand?”, “What remains uncertain?”, “What will I do next?” User-authored only by default. Needs authenticated storage/deletion and opt-in sharing if a practitioner can see it. Do not generate a fake practitioner summary or transcribe/record calls as an implementation shortcut.

### Proposed extension: learn from the person

Curated, named-Pandit reading paths connected to profile and topic pages. Reuse existing publishing infrastructure. Every path must have useful original editorial content; no bulk AI SEO pages.

## Accessibility and interaction

Use semantic headings/forms, persistent labels, clear focus rings, visible error summary and inline field errors, keyboard-operated menus/dialogs and accessible live-region status. Target 44px touch controls. Avoid color-only availability/status. Support 200% zoom, reduced motion, text alternatives for charts and transcripts for introductions. At 360px, booking and payment summaries must not horizontally scroll. Test long Hindi text and enlarged fonts. Important amounts and session controls must remain visible without obstructive sticky elements.

## Human review rubric

Before accepting a page, answer: Who is the real person behind each claim? What can the visitor do here? Is the price understandable? Can they decline or leave? Does the language acknowledge uncertainty? Does the page help with a real question? If the brand logo were removed, would the page still feel specific to this service? Revise any page that is interchangeable with a generic wellness template.
