# Pandit camera vs a dropped video

A missing remote picture is not treated as the pandit turning their camera off.

The pandit app sends whether their camera is on. The seeker shows "Camera off" only after that message says the camera is off. If the picture disappears without that message, the screen says the video is reconnecting.

Both people in the booking can send their own camera state. The message names who sent it, so the seeker only applies the pandit's camera.
