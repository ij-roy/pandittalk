# Daily horoscope reminder

After a daily horoscope opens, the app schedules a local reminder at 8:00 for that sun sign. The title is the sign, for example “Aries today”. If notifications are already allowed, a saved sign or the date-of-birth sun sign is used before the next reading. Tapping the reminder opens the daily horoscope with that sign marked. A sign we do not recognize is not scheduled.
