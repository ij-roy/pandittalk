# Leave and end

Leaving a call or chat does not end it for the other person.

On a call, the red button is labeled Leave. Before you go, it says the pandit stays in the session, your time keeps running, and you can rejoin from My Bookings. The audio call used to label that same button End.

Ending a chat is a separate button. That prompt says it ends the chat for you and the pandit, and that you cannot rejoin.

On the pandit side, Leave asks for confirmation and says the seeker stays. Only the seeker can end the session.
